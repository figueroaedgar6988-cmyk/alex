Hartline Concrete: ten ads, three voices

Each style speaks to a different buyer: contractors who care about specs and schedules, architects who care about finish, and homeowners who want a driveway or patio done right. Hartline Concrete is a placeholder name, and phone numbers use the reserved 555 range.

01 · 4 ADS

Built Heavy

Industrial and blunt, for general contractors and site supers. Safety-yellow on black, condensed poster type, spec-sheet numbers. The promise is reliability: the right mix, on site, on time.

02 · 3 ADS

Quiet Strength

Editorial and architectural, for architects, designers and high-end builds. Warm concrete greys, a light serif, lots of air. The material itself is the hero: form-tie holes, board-formed grain, polished aggregate.

03 · 3 ADS

Home Ground

Warm and friendly, for homeowners planning a driveway, patio or walkway. Rounded shapes, a sky-blue and cream palette with an orange offer, and plain promises: a fast quote, a clean job, a warranty.
