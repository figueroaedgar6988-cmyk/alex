Features

Everything a standup does, minus the meeting

Written updates that fit around deep work and across time zones.

  • Two-minute check-ins

    Three short prompts, sent at each person's local morning. Answer from Slack, email, or the app.

  • Live team board

    See every update in one place, grouped by project, with who's checked in and who hasn't.

  • Blockers surface first

    Anything marked blocked goes to the top and pings the right person, so it doesn't sit for a day.

  • Weekly summaries

    Every Friday, a one-page recap of what shipped, what slipped, and what's next, ready to share.

  • Works with your tools

    Links issues from Jira, Linear, and GitHub automatically, so updates point to real work.

  • Built for time zones

    Nobody has to wake up early for a call. Updates are waiting when each teammate starts their day.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do we have to stop having meetings?

No. Most teams drop the daily standup and keep a weekly sync for discussion. Tidewell handles the status part so meetings can be about decisions.

How long does a check-in take?

About two minutes. The default prompts are what you did, what you're doing next, and whether anything is blocking you.

Can people answer without opening Tidewell?

Yes. People can reply straight from Slack or email, and the answer shows up on the board.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you don't add a payment method, your workspace moves to the free Starter plan. You keep all your history.
